The dome

One dome is one Set. Press it. The film opens, the well floods with Foil Green, the foil ring bursts once and the cell is a record. Pressed cells never return — that is the whole planned-and-actual rule expressed as a material.

States — press any dome

Convex rest is --dome; a pressed cell is --pressed with the hue wash behind it. Never both. A dome that lifts on hover is pressable; a flat recessed cell is history and is not a control.
